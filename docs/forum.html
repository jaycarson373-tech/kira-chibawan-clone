◇Kira rescue story
October 18, 2012, 07:42:16 PM #1

Hello everyone,

I volunteer at Chibawan, a dog rescue organization based in Japan. Together with our partner shelters, we take in dogs from difficult situations every day.

Doge and Neiro were both found around the same time.

Most of the other dogs we rescued were facing euthanasia due to overcrowding and lack of space.
They were all on the list.
Kira, one of the puppies we found that day, was too.

Kira is Doge’s long-lost sister.
She was a little shy and scared at first, but proven to be gentle, kind, and full of love.
Thankfully, Kira was saved just in time.

We are so grateful to everyone who believes every dog deserves a chance.

– Chibawan Volunteer Team
